Request-record lock tests time out on a loaded machine

test/requests.test.ts: "overlapping writers in separate processes lose no update" and the same "when they start on a stale lock" fail with a lock timeout after 90 to 155 s when several gates run on one machine (seen 2026-11-25 on 0077/B and on its base 67719df), while CI passes. The tests wait on real processes under a fixed lock timeout, so local gates fail under load and workers tick the gate box on CI alone. Give the tests a timeout that scales, or fewer processes; recheck once 0077/D caps gates at two per machine.

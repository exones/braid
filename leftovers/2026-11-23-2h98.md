The board reads the PM as stalled while its live checker runs

While the PM's live check of 0070 (#273) ran, the board's programme card said "Stalled: the PM stopped and none of its braids is running · no agent thread, no check, no PR checks pending". The PM launches its checker with `delegate_task`, so the checker is a subagent child thread, and `thread list`, which the board reads (src/board/t3.ts), doesn't list subagent threads. The board's `pmChecks` match on the title `🧭 <repo> pm check: …` therefore never sees a delegated checker; the reviewers have the same blind spot, and the board sees them only because the coordinator records them with `braid review`. Fix: either the PM records its checker the way reviewers are recorded (or as a wait on 0070's request record), or the board reads the PM thread's running child threads.

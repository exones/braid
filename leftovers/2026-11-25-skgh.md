A live request-record lock held longer than 30 s can be taken over as stale

src/braids/state.ts: takeOverStale removes a lock older than LOCK_STALE_MS (30 s) by its mtime. A record write takes 7-9 s on a loaded Windows machine (0078/J measured it), so a slower holder (a slow push) would have its live lock taken over and two writers would run at once, which can lose an update. Not observed yet. Fix: the holder touches the lock between its git calls, or stale is judged by a dead holder pid rather than age.

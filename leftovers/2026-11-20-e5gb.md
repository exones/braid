Board close() doesn't wait for the server's close event

What: `Started.close()` from 0052 waits for the in-flight and queued background reads, but not for the HTTP server's `close` event (interval and watcher teardown).
Where: the board's `startBoardWith` / `Started.close()` in src/.
Why: a later hardening; a test that deletes the folder right after close() could still race the watcher teardown on Windows. Not the cause of the 0052 flake.

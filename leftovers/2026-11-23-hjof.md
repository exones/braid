braid requests commits the carry-over without pushing; cli.md doesn't say so

The PM's live check of #273: a read (`braid requests`) commits the one-time carry-over of .braid/requests.json to the local braid-requests branch and leaves origin behind until the next write. The code calls this intended ("kept local until the next write"), but docs/cli.md says only that `braid requests` fetches the record. Say it in cli.md, or push on the carry-over.

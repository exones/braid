A programme can read stalled beside its braid's question to the user

After 0072/C (PR #343), a programme card lists its braids' user-facing requests (theirs) and sits in Needs you, but the stall check in src/board/programme.ts short-circuits only on forwards, not on theirs. So a programme whose PM is idle while a braid question waits for the user can show stalled next to that question, and the PM may be nudged for something that is the user's. Add theirs to the stall short-circuit, with a test.

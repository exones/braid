A pm/<slug> request is checked and woken while its plan is held

src/board/wake.ts skips a held or paused asking braid, but a request on a programme item (pm/<slug>) is still checked and woken while the PM plan is held (heldPauses in src/board/programme.ts covers braids, not the pm item). Under a programme hold nothing should wake the PM.

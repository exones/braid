A braid held longer than a day never gets its answer wake

After 0072/D (PR #336), the board wakes the asking thread for an answer recorded outside it only within a one-day window after the answer (src/board/wake.ts, the answer cut-off). A wake held back while the braid is held or paused for more than a day is dropped for good on resume, so the coordinator never learns the PM answered. Count the window from the resume, or skip the window for held braids.

Two braids can take the same number

braid new picks the next number from the branches it can see after a fetch, so two coordinators planning at the same time both get it: a plan that is only committed locally is invisible to the other. On 2026-11-19 "Autonomy presets" (planned 18:19, pushed 18:25) and "Board: item details in a side panel" (planned 18:21) both became 0034. Nothing notices until both are on one branch, where braid check fails on the duplicate id, and by then each has branches, threads and PRs under the number. Where: newBraid in src/commands/edit.ts and src/braids/numbers.ts. A fix could reserve the number on origin at once (as braid note pushes to braid-inbox), or braid check and braid list --open could warn about a duplicate number across branches as soon as both are pushed.

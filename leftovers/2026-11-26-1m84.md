A story or final PR can still set the preset it is judged by in its own brief

Since 0078/D (PR #352), braid autonomy and braid accept --preset read the presets and the BRAID.md default from origin/<base>, so a PR cannot loosen autonomy/*.md or BRAID.md. But a story's or a goal's braid brief is not on main until its own PR merges, so that PR still supplies its own autonomy: field and ## Autonomy section (src/commands/autonomy.ts falls back to the live brief). Closing it needs the approved preset recorded outside the PR, for example on the braid-requests record when the plan is approved, and read from there. Rest of 2026-11-20-w5cd (1).

braid new counts open braids by stored status, not statusOf

After 0075/B, an item's status comes from statusOf (src: the record's lifecycle events, its PR, then the stored status), but loadOpenBraids still reads the stored status in brief.md. A story merged under the new CLI keeps a stored To Do or In Progress, so braid new (and anything else using loadOpenBraids) over-counts it as open. Route loadOpenBraids through statusOf, or accept PR state there too.

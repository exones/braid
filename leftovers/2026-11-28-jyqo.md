braid-pm and braid skills: Hold still describes forwarding by hand; a signed-in board now stops threads

Since 0080/E (merged into braid/0080, in main with 0080's final PR), a running board signed in to T3 acts on a `braid pause <n> --hold` record within about 30 s: it stops each working thread of the braid with t3_thread_interrupt stop:true (children with it), sends the hold text and counts Held: replies (docs/board.md "Stop before the words"). The braid-pm skill's "Hold" section and the braid skill's autonomy "Hold" still have the PM and coordinators forward the hold to every subagent by hand. Once the user has signed the board in, those steps could shrink to "record the hold; check the card's still-working line". A skill change, so the user's to ask for.

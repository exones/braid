Inbox writes give up after 10 s waiting for the lock
braid note and braid inbox take|pass|drop|reopen take the lock .braid/inbox.lock in the main checkout through locked() (src/braids/requests.ts: LOCK_WAIT_MS = 10_000; a lock older than 30 s is taken over). A write that holds it longer, for example several refused pushes retried with fetches on a slow network (write() in src/inbox/branch.ts, up to 5 attempts), makes a second writer on the same machine fail with a lock error. Nothing is lost and running the command again works, but a coordinator running a sweep (several commands in a row) next to a worker capturing may see spurious failures. Consider a longer wait for the inbox lock than for requests.

Quiet until <time> for a PM or coordinator, without asking the user

A PM or coordinator that knows it will be idle on purpose until a set time (for example waiting on something the board cannot see) has no way to tell the board without opening a request that lands on the user: braid ask pm/<slug> --to is refused ("a PM request is already the user's"), and no --done-when fits. A small "quiet until <iso>" record (or a --done-when time:<iso> wait allowed on pm/<slug>) that suppresses the nudge and stall until then, and never notifies the user, would cover it. Where: src/commands/requests.ts (ask), src/board/programme.ts (holds), src/board/model.ts (coordinator stall). Not built in 0075/A (brief: only if it fell out naturally).

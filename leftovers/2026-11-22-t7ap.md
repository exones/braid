braid wait: a one-read mode for the coordinator's short CI reads

Since 0068 (D91) the coordinator reads the braid branch's CI with `braid wait <number> --timeout 0.25`, but src/commands/wait.ts still sleeps POLL_MS (15 s) after a non-terminal first poll, so the "short" read blocks about 15 s.
A `--once` flag (one read, then answer) would make it instant. A read whose output already names a failed check could also exit 1 instead of 3, so the coordinator needs no rule to spot a failure behind "still running".

Top-level workers started before 0060 can re-read the new worker skill

Braids whose workers were launched as top-level threads before the 0060 skills: if such a worker re-reads braid-worker after the new skill is installed (resume, compaction, a pause), it stops waiting on CI and stops sending its report with t3_thread_send. Nothing then wakes its coordinator, because a top-level thread has no task result. The skills (.agents/skills/braid-worker/SKILL.md, braid SKILL "Run an item") could say one line: a top-level worker reports with t3_thread_send and watches its PR as before. Or install the new skills only once no top-level worker runs.

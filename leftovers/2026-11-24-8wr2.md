A stale pendingBackgroundTasks entry could keep a thread counted as running forever

Since 0075/G (PR #303), working() in src/board/t3.ts counts a thread as running while T3 lists any pendingBackgroundTasks or blockedOnThreadWait, with no cap. If T3 ever leaves a stale entry on a finished thread, the board never nudges or stalls that item or PM again. The PM checker records got a 24 h cap in 0075/A; nothing similar bounds this. Check what T3 does with a background task whose session ended, and add a cap (for example the task's age) if it can go stale.

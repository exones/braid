Refresh a live request-record lock so it is not taken over as stale

The record lock (lockedBranch, src/braids/gitbranch.ts) is taken over as stale after 30 s. A write that takes longer on a saturated machine (seen up to 7.5 s per write in 0077/H, longer in 0078/J) can lose its lock to a waiting writer while it still runs; the two lock tests in test/requests.test.ts can then fail. Touching the lock while the write runs (a heartbeat) would keep a live holder from looking stale.

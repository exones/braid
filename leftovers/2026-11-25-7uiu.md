braid clean leaves a subagent worker's item branch until the strand is named

Seen on the 0060/D live trial, from the braid/0060 build. `braid clean 0060 --yes` removed the worker child's T3 checkout and t3/ branch, then skipped the item: "worktree C:/worktrees/agento/t3-… is not in the braid worktree folder (name the strand to clean it)". It names the T3 checkout it had just removed, and it kept the local branch braid/0060/D-…. Only `braid clean 0060/D --yes` removed the branch. src/commands/clean.ts should treat the item branch of a recorded worker child as its own: remove the branch, and drop the skip line, once its recorded checkout is gone.

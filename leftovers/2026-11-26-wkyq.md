Count the probe's time in the service start wait

After 0077/C (PR #353) probeBoard waits up to 10 s. In service install and restart (src/commands/service.ts) the wait-for-start loop's until budget counts only its 250 ms sleeps, not the probes, so a failed start while another address file's port also hangs can run far past the intended 30 s. Measure the budget by the clock (Date.now) instead of by sleeps.

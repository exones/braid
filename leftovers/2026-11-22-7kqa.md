A limit pause on a held braid replaces the hold

What: a braid has one pause record, so braid pause --provider on a braid held with braid pause --hold replaces the hold; the scheduled resume at the window reset then runs braid resume and can restart work the user or PM held.
Why: a deliberate hold must outlive a rate-limit pause.
Where: the pause store and braid pause/resume (0061/C, extended in 0062/B). Fix: refuse a limit pause over a hold, or keep the hold under it and make resume clear only the limit.

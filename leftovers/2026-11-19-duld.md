Flaky temp-folder cleanup in tests on Windows

The teardown hook of test/t3.test.ts (line 10) deletes its temp folder with rmSync(scratch, { recursive: true, force: true }), and on the Windows runner it failed once with EPERM (a process still held the folder). The whole verify (windows-latest) job went red on a docs-only PR (#164 at cdb7f02) and passed on a re-run. Adding maxRetries (and retryDelay) to that rmSync, and to the other temp cleanups in test/, would stop a red CI that blocks auto-mode merges for no reason.

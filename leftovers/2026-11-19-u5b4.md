A cancelled CI check counts as green

runsOf in src/board/github.ts treats the check conclusions CANCELLED, ACTION_REQUIRED and STALE as passing. braid accept, braid handover and the board read checks through it, so a `ci` check that was cancelled (a superseded or manually stopped run) can let a PR be accepted, handed over or merged as green. braid wait (0034/A, src/commands/wait.ts) decides for itself and passes only success, neutral and skipped; the shared reader should do the same, with a test per conclusion.

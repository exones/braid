Pause text asks a subagent worker for a CI result it no longer has

The worker pause text (src/board/steer.ts workerPause, and the braid-worker Habits line and references/limits.md, owned by 0074) says a worker woken during a pause replies briefly with its item's CI result. Since 0060/C a worker is a delegate_task child that never waits on CI: the coordinator watches the item PR. So that clause is dead for subagent workers. Drop it, or limit it to top-level workers, when the pause text is next edited (0074 or later).

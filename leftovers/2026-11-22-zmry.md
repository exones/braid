A failed one-shot limit push can still be lost on a multi-project board

What: in src/notify/notifier.ts the limit pusher's retry() drops the waiting entry while a send is still in flight, so a one-shot paused, failed or resumed push whose send then fails is not retried.
Where: src/notify/notifier.ts (limitPusher retry and waiting), braid 0064/C.
Why: the pause, failure and resume pushes are the user's only signal from a board that pauses on its own.

braid board --host: the phone URL changes every run

The Tailscale listener of braid board --host (src/commands/board.ts, src/board/tailnet.ts) takes the local port when free there, else a new free port, and the local port is itself random per run, so a bookmark on the phone breaks after every restart of the board. A --port option (or a remembered port in .braid/) would give a stable phone URL.

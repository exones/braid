scripts/Publish-Npm.ps1, the local publish path, has no guard

Found in 0078/B (PR #342): the release workflow is guarded by test/pester/Release-Workflow.Tests.ps1 (OIDC, no token, dispatch only), but scripts/Publish-Npm.ps1, which publishes from a local machine with a token taken from Infisical, is checked by nothing. A change there (a different registry, no --ignore-scripts, publishing more than one tarball) would go unnoticed. A small Pester suite over the script, or retiring the local path in favour of the workflow, would close it.

0082/A mockups: the Questions panel

Test data only: three projects (sample, api, shop), nine questions to answer (one of them a PR to merge) and two looks or FYIs. Every frame shows the same data. The page follows your system theme; ◐ switches it. The board today is light only, so the dark version shows the design, not a promise of a dark board. The context and options fields come with 0082/C.

1. The whole board at desktop width: programmes left, lanes, Questions panel right, open

New against today: the column on the right, 416 px (programmes: 320 px), always showing every open question of every project, ranked. It scrolls by itself and stays on screen while the lanes scroll. The header, lanes, cards and programmes panel are as today, except that a programme card's footer now says the panel can answer too. Below about 1600 px, opening one side panel collapses the other to its rail. A waiting question also stays on its card, also on a Done braid's. Drawn 1600 px wide and scaled to your window. The tooltip on » is drawn open.

2. The same board with the Questions panel collapsed: its rail

New: a 56 px rail on the right, like the programmes rail on the left. It shows the count of questions to answer (9; looks and FYIs aren't counted), and a red ! mark while one is escalated. A click on either opens the panel. Open or collapsed is remembered in the browser.

3. The list, ranked by what each question blocks

New: one list instead of need lines spread over cards, programme cards and each item's Requests tab. Order: escalated; stops a braid or programme entirely; blocks several items (more first); blocks one item; then, collapsed, requests marked on the record as a look or an FYI (a new mark, 0082/C). A request that blocks nothing but needs a decision stays in the ranked list. A PR waiting for your merge is ranked like any question, with its PR link and the Merge button (⇥, the same confirmation as on the card). Oldest first within a rank. Each row: handle (click copies, as today), the wait, escalation mark, the question, who asks with the project, blocks … · decide|do. A click on a row opens it in place.

4. One question opened in place: the roomy card

New: the row grows into the card of 0072's mockup, in the panel: the context, shown as the card opens (a click folds it), the question in larger type, one button per option the agent proposed, and ✎ (Custom answer) which swaps the buttons for a one-line field. One click on an option sends the answer at once, with no undo, and the next question in rank order opens by itself.

5. A question with no proposed options (an older request)

New: a request without options shows only the free-text line with Send. Without context there is no context box; the line under the question links the item instead. A "do" request is answered the same way: say what you did.

6. Decided: collapsed by default, then opened

New: one Decided group for all projects, newest first: answered questions of braids and of programmes (a PM's own request, here tiny-tractor-3i2b, which today shows nowhere). Each shows the question, the answer, who answered (you, or the PM with its reason) and when. Ten at first, then Show more.

The option buttons, Custom and the field are drawn for the layout: answering from the board comes with P25 (The Questions panel). The built card in 0072/B shows the context and the question only; the buttons appear when they work.

Where context and options come from (proposal; a record change, not built until confirmed)

Two added optional fields on the request record, like words in 0072/G; no migration, a record without them shows no Context section and no options:

context: a text, markdown allowed, the background needed to understand the question.
options: a list of { letter, text }, letters A, B, C… in order.

Flags on braid ask: --context <text> (or --context-file <path|-> for long text) and --option <text>, repeatable; the letters follow the order given (--option "Half-up: …" --option "Banker's: …"). The board reads both into RequestRow and the details card; the CLI's braid requests <handle> prints them too. Answers to an option are recorded as the letter plus the text.

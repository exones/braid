## 2026-11-26 06:34Z

Decided: the queue test no longer races a holder process: `locked` takes an optional clock (now, pause) and the test hands the lock over from the waiter's pause. Why: under 24 busy processes the timed holder (20 ms naps, 4 s wait, 12 s queue) still failed 1 of 10 runs, and a diagnosis run saw 15.7 s between two hand-offs in 1 of 40, so no margin is safe. The lock now holds a random token that is part of its identity: on Linux a new lock reused the inode in 2999 of 3000 tries (WSL, ext4), leaving only file times to tell holds apart. (521a6d98)

Colours follow the provider chips: green-tinted when signed in, amber when ending, red when refused or ended, dashed grey when simply not signed in, and faint dashed (no dot, not clickable) when this host has no secret store. "Refused" covers a 401 (revoked or ended in T3) and a token T3 doesn't take; the board never retries that token, and turns signed in again only when the store holds a new one. A T3 that can't be reached isn't "signed out": the button keeps its state and the existing "T3 unavailable" header note says so.

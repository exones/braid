Recording a merge answer must not move the PR head

What: when the user picks Merge now on a handover, the coordinator first runs braid answer <id> --log - and commits that log entry on the braid branch, as the skill says ("A reply to an open request: first braid answer, then act"). On a final PR the braid branch is the PR head, so that commit moves the head away from the recapped one, and braid accept --merge --head <recapped sha> then refuses ("PR head is <new>, not the reviewed <sha>"). Seen on braid 0064, PR #251, 2026-11-23: the answer commit cd23a64 moved the head from 812ce8e and blocked the merge the user had asked for.
Where: the braid skill ("Merge on request", "A reply to an open request"), braid answer, braid accept --merge (src/commands/done.ts or near it).
Why: the user's merge request fails for a reason they never see, and the coordinator must ask again for a head that differs only by a log line.
Options: braid accept --merge takes the answer log entry itself and commits it after the merge (like its --log), or braid answer writes the log without committing when the next step is a merge, or braid accept accepts a head whose commits since the recapped one touch only braids/.

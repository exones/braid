Wait on another braid's completion

Let the user put a braid, or one strand, on hold until another braid is Done, and have braid honour that: the waiting item isn't started, the board shows it as waiting and names the braid it waits for, and it becomes ready again once that braid is Done. Briefs already have a `dependencies` field that accepts braid ids, but today only `braid next` and `braid start` read it, for strands; this idea makes "waits for braid X" an explicit, visible state across the board, `braid list` and the coordinator.

Related: `dependencies` and `related` fields (docs/braids-format.md), `braid next`, `braid start`, the board's lanes (0006, 0015), 0027 Planning looks at the other open braids

Open questions:
- Is this the existing `dependencies` field shown and enforced everywhere, or a separate "on hold" marker set at any time, even on a braid already in progress?
- Should a strand be able to wait on a braid from another number, or a braid on a strand?
- When the awaited braid is Done, does the waiting item just become ready, or does braid tell the user or start it in auto mode?
- How is it set: a `braid edit` option, a board action, or both?

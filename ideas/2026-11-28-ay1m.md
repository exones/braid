braid t3 login/status/logout always use the real OS secret store entry (service braid, account t3), even with a scratch HOME/USERPROFILE, so tests and live checks can't fully isolate `braid t3` (PM check of #391, 2026-11-28: logout called delete on the real entry; none existed). A BRAID_SECRET_SERVICE (or BRAID_HOME-derived) service name, or an env to point the store at a scratch backend, would let checkers run the signed-in path without touching the user's real sign-in.

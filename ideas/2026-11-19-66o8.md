Autonomy levels for braids

Let the user set how much braid does on its own, per braid or for the work as a whole, from "ask me before each step" up to "merge everything yourself". Today there is one switch, auto mode, which merges a goal's strand PRs into its braid branch but never into main; this would turn it into a scale the user picks, with a level where braid also merges into main.

Related: 0001 Auto mode: braid merges phases into the epic branch; .agents/skills/braid/references/auto-mode.md; AGENTS.md hard limits (merges into main are the user's)

- Does "merge everything" still wait for green CI and review, and which stops always reach the user?

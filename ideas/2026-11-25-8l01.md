Question codes that can be said aloud: an adjective and a noun ("yellow bear", "purple chair") instead of q-5asx.

The user, 2026-11-25: "I use voice typing a lot like 90% of the time... naming questions with adjective plus noun would be great, meaning that if I said the question yellow bear or purple chair... would be much much easier."

Shape:
- Two built-in word lists of about 100–150 short, common, distinct-sounding words each. No dependency needed; npm `unique-names-generator` and `human-id` do the same, but a list in the repo is deterministic and reviewable.
- Exclude homophones and near-homophones (bear/bare, pair/pear, blue/blew, right/write), words a speech engine splits or capitalises oddly, and anything rude.
- Deterministic: picked from a hash of the request id, re-drawn on a clash with an open or recently answered request.
- Every place that takes a code accepts it loosely: "yellow bear", "Yellow Bear.", "yellow-bear", "yellowbear", any case, trailing punctuation. Old q-codes keep working.
- The board, pushes and chat show the words first.
- In chat an answer reads naturally: "yellow bear a".

braid handover cannot hand over a port PR into main

In braid 0078 the coordinator opened early PRs into main that carry one merged strand's change alone (#335, #384, #395). braid handover <id> --head <sha> --to pm looks only at the item's own PR (for 0078/O the merged #394) and refuses ("PR #394 is merged; nothing changed"), so the PM cannot forward a handover and the coordinator falls back to braid ask --kind action. A --pr <n> flag on braid handover (checked open, at that head, green) would let a port PR go through the normal handover and forward.

Path-scoped gates, braid doctor, worktree caches and disk space

Field report, Everix monorepo (2026-11-23). (5) The full gate takes ~23 minutes and needs submodules, a per-worktree NuGet cache, a Vault token and a particular pnpm/corepack setup; it broke three ways on a dev machine (Git Bash tar first on PATH, the pinned pnpm binary not linked, corepack missing under Volta) before passing; docs-only or frontend-only changes don't need the .NET build. Wanted: `gate:` as a list of `<glob> → <command>` with the full gate as fallback; `braid doctor` that runs `setup` and a dry gate once and reports environment failures as setup problems, not item failures. (6) Each worker worktree needs a submodule init and a ~7 GB private cache on a nearly full disk, and `braid clean` doesn't remove caches. Wanted: a `cleanup:` paths field that `braid clean` also removes, and a disk-space check before `braid start`.

Related: BRAID.md gate/setup, braid start, braid clean, P14 (slow clean/start), P18 (braid init).

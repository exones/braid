A PM can't wait on purpose until a time: `braid ask pm/<slug> ... --to self --done-when time:<iso>` is refused ("a PM request is already the user's"), so a PM that waits for the user's morning (user asked for quiet until 10:00) gets "Nudge from the board: the PM ... looks idle" even though forwarded requests (0074 handover, 0080/D and 0080/G questions) are open with the user. Fix: allow --to self on pm/<slug> (an idle-until-time wait), and/or count requests the PM forwarded to the user as the programme's open requests in the stall check.

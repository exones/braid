T3 v0.1.10-0.1.12 (installed: v0.1.12, 2026-11-25) changes that matter for braid, from a release review (scratchpad r10-12.md): (1) MCP Events (thread.run_settled, needs_input, pr.watch_wake; poll/stream; 1,000-event in-memory window, `truncated` after restart; OAuth clients only) can replace the board's 30 s CLI poll, with a slow full read as backstop; (2) OAuth sign-in for outside agents lasts 30 days with no refresh: the board must warn before expiry; Supervised can start, message and stop threads; (3) a parent's Stop now stops its delegate_task children (#34499, #41860): test whether interrupting a coordinator cascades to its workers/reviewers, which would fix Hold not reaching children; (4) sends now un-settle threads (#33995); `disable_auto_settle` exists for long-running PM/coordinator threads; (5) T3 storage cleanup now removes merged subagent worktrees and slow ones (#41694, #42034): check braid clean doesn't fight it; (6) T3 has its own usage-limit hold and auto-resume (v0.1.10): check it doesn't double-act with braid's 90% pause; (7) CLI state-changing commands go through the running server (v0.1.11): `t3 thread send` fails when T3 is down; (8) T3 Workflows (beta, Labs): durable TS workflows with schedules, triggers, ask, store; a spike could show what of braid's scheduling, stall wake and approvals could run there.

React to remote review bots (CodeRabbit, Grok automation)

When a repo already has review bots on every PR (CodeRabbit, a Cursor Grok automation), braid reacts to them as part of the item's loop: after the PR opens, the worker (or the coordinator) waits for the bots' reviews, reads every comment, triages each (fix it, answer why not, or mark a nit), pushes the fixes, replies on the comment and resolves the thread, and the braid reviewer reads the bots' comments and the replies instead of reviewing from scratch. The bots are named in BRAID.md (which checks or comment authors they are), so `braid wait` knows to wait for them and their checks don't count as CI. Goes beyond 0073's "name them as input to the braid reviewer or excluded from ci": this is acting on what they say.

Why: on Everix both bots already review every PR; today braid ignores their comments, so findings sit unanswered and the braid reviewer duplicates them.

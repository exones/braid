A hold doesn't hold. Audit of the 2026-11-25 hold for the T3 restart: every coordinator reported "held", yet about 10 threads went on working. The causes:
1. Coordinators confirmed before their workers' turns ended. Sends queue; the SFIL PM said "held" 5 min before its 0014/B worker stopped.
2. A message queued before the hold lands after it. 0010/F said "Held", then got a 14:42 "URGENT" message and debugged for 6 more minutes.
3. Wakes ignore the hold. A PR-watch wake made a held worker (dctl 0013/C) send its final report. Background gate runs kept going.
4. A hold covers only the braids it lists. The unlisted everix 0008 was resumed and nudged by the board, and its coordinator relaunched the worker.
5. Delegated reviewers and background processes never receive the hold.

Wanted (with P5's Hold and Go, and 0074's pause by code): one "Hold all" from the board or CLI.
- It records the hold per project, so the board never resumes or nudges a held braid.
- It sends to every working thread and its children, by code (steer, once available).
- It counts "Held" replies from the threads themselves, not from coordinators.
- It shows who is still working, and declares "all held" only when T3 shows no running thread of those braids.
- Workers treat the hold as stronger than any queued or woken work.

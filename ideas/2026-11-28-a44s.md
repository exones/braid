The every-third-merge decisions digest is easy to miss

In braid 0078 (free preset, PM-run) the coordinator merged ten strands itself and missed the digest that autonomy.md "Telling" asks for after every third merge twice (due after B and after L; sent late after C and after E). Nothing reminds the coordinator: braid accept --merge --preset could print "digest due: braid decisions <n> --since <last>" when the count since the last digest reaches three, or keep the count on the record, so the rule needs no memory.

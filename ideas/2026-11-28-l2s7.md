braid new should reserve its braid number on origin

On 2026-11-28 three coordinators planning in parallel (several open questions per item, board lanes and Held lane, braid clean with submodules) each got 0084 from braid new on their own unpushed plan branch, since none could see the others' numbers. One plan request then replaced another's (same item, asker and sort; q-um22 withdrawn by q-azs7), and the PM had to reassign 0084, 0085, 0086 by hand. braid new (src/commands/braids.ts) could reserve the number with one commit on a shared branch such as braid-requests, pushed at once, and skip numbers reserved there, so parallel planners never collide.

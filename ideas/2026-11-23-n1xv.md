CI that differs per PR, partial CI on braid branches, review bots

Field report from setting up braid on a large polyglot monorepo (Everix, 2026-11-23). (1) The checks on a PR depend on the paths it changes (TeamCity routed skip, ~25 path-filtered Woodpecker pipelines), so a fixed `ci:` list doesn't fit; `ci: []` makes `braid wait` exit at once and the merge condition "checks green" a no-op. Wanted: `ci: all` (wait for every check reported on the commit, with a start grace), or path rules (`<glob> → <names>`), or required names plus anything reported. (2) Full CI runs only on PRs into main; strand PRs into braid/* get partial CI, so a merging preset merges strands on partial CI. Wanted: BRAID.md can say CI on braid branches is partial or none, and the worker's gate run (output and duration) becomes the required strand evidence. (8) Review bots (CodeRabbit, a Cursor automation) already review every PR and show as checks: name them, as input to the braid reviewer or excluded from `ci`.

Related: BRAID.md `ci`, braid wait, braid accept, the braid-reviewer skill.
